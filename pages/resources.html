Learning resources

Simple guides you can return to before or after a speaking activity.

✎

Prepare a short outline

Write your main idea, two supporting details, and one closing thought. Use the outline as a guide rather than memorizing every word.

◷

Use a comfortable pace

Speak slowly enough for listeners to follow. Short pauses are useful when you need time to organize your next thought.

◎

Make your message clear

State your point directly, explain unfamiliar ideas, and choose examples that match your audience.

♡

Respond kindly to mistakes

Notice what happened, continue when you can, and choose one specific thing to practice next time.

↗

Connect with your listener

Think about what your listener needs to understand. Use relevant reasons and examples to support your message.

☷

Reflect after practicing

Ask yourself what went well, what felt difficult, and what small change you want to try during your next practice session.
